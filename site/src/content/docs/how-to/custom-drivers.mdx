> The full porting guide is maintained in Simplified Chinese: [编写自定义硬件与传输驱动](/wirelink/zh-cn/how-to/custom-drivers/).

Port Wirelink to custom transports via `wl_packet_port_t` (for packet-framed links) or `wl_packet_stream_t` + `wl_byte_fifo_t` (for UART and raw byte streams). Also see [docs/porting-adapters.md](https://github.com/starwey604/wirelink/blob/main/docs/porting-adapters.md).
