A canonical native/NONE DATA packet has a four-byte Compact-v1 prefix and payload. RPC payload adds client instance, operation identity and rejection status metadata before its managed tagged body.

The [packet/RPC reference](https://github.com/starwey604/wirelink/blob/main/docs/packet-protocol.md) defines exact bytes, lengths, validation and frozen vectors. Keep these layers separate:

- **Public Schema-v1:** tagged standalone/ordinary codec, full legal field domains.
- **Ordinary positional-v1:** explicitly selected schema-dependent message encoding.
- **Managed tagged RPC:** instance/operation metadata and private business codec; scalar fixed32 representation differs from public Schema-v1.
- **Connector:** native/NONE, COBS/CRC16 or length16/NONE around canonical packets.

Peers agree on message IDs, codec and connector out of band. Instance identity correlates calls; integrity detects corruption. Authentication and business idempotency need their own mechanisms. See [schema/profile generation](/wirelink/reference/schema-and-binding/) and [transport boundaries](/wirelink/reference/transports/).
